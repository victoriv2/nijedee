NIJEDEE primarily covers the Lekki, Ajah, Badore and Sangotedo axis. Other parts of Lagos (including Victoria Island, Ikoyi and mainland commercial hubs) are served by arrangement.
After submitting your request through our online booking engine, you will receive an instant confirmation message. A NIJEDEE representative will contact you via WhatsApp or phone to confirm the details, provide the itemized quote after inspection, and assign a vetted artisan.
Our marketplace provides authentic Nijedee Paints (Emulsion, Matt, Higher Satin), building materials (cement, tiles, plumbing/electrical conduits, POP materials), professional tools and equipment, and property security/solar products.
