Why NIJEDEE Verified Dispatch is Safe

We do not publish fabricated profiles or unvetted contractors. Every artisan working under NIJEDEE goes through our strict 4-step compliance check.

1. Identity & NIN Verification

National Identity Number (NIN) authentication, valid residential address physical check, and two verified guarantors on record.

2. Practical Trade Testing

Hands-on diagnostic assessment conducted at the NIJEDEE Vocational Academy to verify real tool handling, safety, and workmanship.

3. Fixed Standard Pricing

Zero artisan haggling or inflated charges. Diagnostic rates and material quotations follow standardized Lagos market benchmarks.

4. Workmanship Guarantee

Every dispatch job is monitored by a NIJEDEE supervisor. If a repair fails within our warranty period, it is rectified without labor cost.
