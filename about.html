Our Story & Purpose

Built to Eliminate Frustration in Property Care

For years, property owners, corporate managers, and residential tenants across Lagos have faced the persistent dilemma of unreliable technicians, inflated material prices, poor craftsmanship, and zero accountability.

NIJEDEE was established as an integrated corporate enterprise to eliminate these pain points. By combining 9 specialized business divisions under one roof, we provide end-to-end management where every artisan is verified, every material is certified genuine, and every job is backed by a satisfaction guarantee.

Our 9 Business Arms

A multi-service ecosystem creating comprehensive value for homeowners, businesses, and communities.

Arm 01

Property Maintenance & Repairs
