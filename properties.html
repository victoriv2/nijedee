Blueprint Scope

What Property Management Includes

Our facility management contracts cover the full lifecycle of property upkeep, ensuring your property value continues to appreciate without operational headaches.

Routine Inspections

Scheduled structural, electrical, plumbing, and roof checks to catch issues before they turn into costly damages.

Preventive Maintenance

Proactive servicing of air conditioning, water pumps, generators, borehole systems, and drainage lines.

Cleaning & Fumigation

Deep cleaning for tenancy transitions, ongoing common-area hygiene, post-construction cleanup, and quarterly pest control.

Prompt Repairs

On-demand dispatch of verified plumbers, electricians, carpenters, and painters at pre-approved transparent labor rates.

Tenant Support

A single dedicated communication channel for tenants to report maintenance issues, resolved with minimum turnaround time.

Property Reporting

Detailed periodic physical condition reports and expense summaries delivered straight to the landlord's dashboard or WhatsApp.
