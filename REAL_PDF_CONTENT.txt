01 \001 Executive Summary
NIJEDEE is a technology-driven property maintenance, repair, cleaning, facility management and
property marketplace company serving homeowners, tenants, landlords, businesses and
property managers across Lagos, with primary coverage in Lekki, Ajah, Badore and Sangotedo.
From fixing a leaking pipe and servicing an AC unit, to deep cleaning, painting, buying building
materials and managing an entire property, NIJEDEE brings property services into one platform
\001 reducing the friction, risk and guesswork customers currently face when sourcing trustworthy
artisans and materials.

The Opportunity
Property problems in Lagos are stressful: finding a trustworthy artisan, getting a fair price,
sourcing materials, and supervising repairs each currently require separate, unreliable
channels. NIJEDEE consolidates these into one trusted platform \001 a strategy that mirrors
successful home-services marketplaces globally, adapted to the realities and trust deficit of the
Nigerian informal artisan economy.
Four Pillars, One Platform
Services
On-demand booking of 13 categories of home and office repair, maintenance and
cleaning work, delivered by verified professionals.
Marketplace
NIJEDEE Shop for building materials, paints, tools and property products \001 with
cart, checkout and delivery tracking.
Properties
Shortlets, rentals, sales listings and full property management for landlords and
property owners.
People
A two-sided artisan network plus the Vocational Skills Academy \001 training the
next generation of verified professionals.

11 \001 Investor View \003 Business Model
How the platform's modules translate into revenue.
Revenue Stream
Mechanism
Services (core)
Commission or margin on 13 booked service categories, priced per job after
inspection/quote.
Shop (marketplace)
Retail margin on building materials, Nijedee-branded paints, tools and
property products.
Properties
Booking fees on shortlets, listing/agency fees on rentals & sales, recurring
facility-management contracts.
Artisan Network
Lead-generation or subscription fees from verified artisans; premium
placement in the directory.
Academy
Programme fees from the two-week vocational training cycle; graduates
feed the artisan supply pipeline.
Why This Model Compounds
Each pillar reinforces the others. A services customer becomes a shop customer buying
materials for the same job. A shortlet guest may become a long-term property client. A
facility-management contract generates recurring service bookings. Every completed job trains
the ratings data that makes the artisan network more trustworthy \001 and the Academy supplies
the verified workforce that scales the whole system without relying purely on the existing
informal artisan market.
Market Context
NIJEDEE currently serves Lekki, Ajah, Badore and Sangotedo, with other parts of Lagos served
by arrangement \001 a dense, fast-growing corridor of new residential and commercial
development where reliable, verified property services are in persistent short supply.
